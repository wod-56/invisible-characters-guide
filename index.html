How to Use Invisible Space for Blank Names in Free Fire

Discover how to create blank names in Free Fire using espaço invisível (invisible space). A simple, safe, and effective step-by-step guide.

What Is Invisible Space?

The term invisible space, or espaço invisível, refers to a Unicode character that looks like a blank space but is not a regular spacebar input. These special characters are used to make text appear invisible on digital platforms such as Free Fire, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Discord.

When you use an invisible space, it allows you to create names or messages that look empty, but are still recognized by the system as valid text. This is why players often use these characters to create blank nicknames in Free Fire.

Tip: Invisible characters are commonly used from Unicode points like U+3164 (Hangul Filler) or Zero Width Space (ZWSP).

Step-by-Step: How to Use Invisible Space for Blank Names

  1. Visit the trusted site espaço invisível.
  2. Copy the invisible character from the website.
  3. Open Free Fire and go to your profile settings.
  4. Click on the “Change Name” option.
  5. Paste the invisible space character into the text field.
  6. Save your changes and confirm — your name will appear blank!

How Invisible Space Works Technically

Invisible spaces use Unicode characters that have no visible width or shape. These include:

  • Zero Width Space (U+200B)
  • Zero Width Non-Joiner (U+200C)
  • Hangul Filler (U+3164)

When pasted into a text field, the game recognizes these as valid input, even though they render nothing on screen. That’s why Free Fire allows them as part of player names.
